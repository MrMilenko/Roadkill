**Roadkill 0.2.1: Dark Tooth, the hidden cars, and the hidden arenas.**

Everything new here is an optional switch in `roadkill.ini`, off by default: with nothing switched on, Roadkill plays exactly like the 1997 PC game.

**No game data is included.** You need your own Twisted Metal 2 PC CD (`import_disc.py` sets it up; see `README.txt`).

## New switches

Add them under `[gameplay]` in `roadkill.ini`:

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| `unlock_hidden_cars = 1` | **Minion** and **Sweet Tooth** on the car select from the start, through the game's own unlock (the one the ICECREAM / BIGBOY cheat codes trigger). |
| `play_dark_tooth = 1` | **Dark Tooth** as a playable car: his own slot on the wheel (after Minion) with his own model, shrunk to car size in play. He uses Sweet Tooth's portrait, info page, ending and tournament progression, as the game has none of his own. Works in network games too (every player needs the switch on). |
| `hidden_arenas = 1` | Network games: the host's arena arrows also reach **Florida : Suicide Swamp** (a Jet Moto crossover arena SingleTrac hid in the PC game) and the two Twisted Metal 1 arenas, **Los Angeles : Cyburbia** and **Los Angeles : Roof Tops**. Only the host needs it; joiners play them without it. |

## Fixes to the original

- The network Session Setup screen now shows each arena's picture: the 1997 game asked for `SC-<arena>.PCX` but the CD's files are named `SC_<arena>.PCX`, so it never showed one.
- A window repaint the game relies on now happens as it did on Windows.

## Secrets, documented

The PC game's debug cheat codes, decoded in `docs/QOL.md`: in a network game, the host can type **DASBAK** (debug mode), then **JMSWAMP**, **TMBURBS** or **TMROOF** on Session Setup to reach the hidden arenas without any switch, the way the original intended. **SLEUTH** is a spectator camera.

## Known limits

- Dark Tooth: when you play as him, the final Dark Tooth fight is skipped (the game can't have two Dark Tooths at once; the original does the same with Minion in New York). Split screen and his special weapon are untested.
- The hidden arenas are network only, as in the original; challenge and tournament modes there are untested.
